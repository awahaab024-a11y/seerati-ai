AI Resume Builder for Arabic and English Resumes

Seerati AI turns your raw experience into a structured resume. Enter what you have done, paste the job you want, and the AI rewrites your bullet points in clear business English or Modern Standard Arabic. It is instructed to use only the facts you provide, so you stay in control of what your resume claims.

Benefits

  • Bilingual output with correct right-to-left layout for Arabic
  • Four print-ready A4 templates, including a minimal ATS layout
  • Keyword match and gap analysis against a job description
  • Section-level editing with undo, redo, shorten, expand and translate

How it works

  1. Fill in your details step by step.
  2. Paste the target job description.
  3. Generate, then review every line.
  4. Pick a template and export.

Who is it for?

Job seekers who apply in both Arabic and English markets, graduates writing a first resume, and professionals tailoring one profile to several roles. If you only need a simple template, the minimal layout works without any AI at all.

What to prepare

  • Your job history with accurate start and end months
  • What you actually did in each role, plus numbers you can back up
  • Education, certificates and links to your work
  • The full text of the job you are applying for

How to review the AI output

Read every bullet and ask whether it really happened, whether the number is right, and whether you could explain it in an interview. Remove anything you would not be comfortable being asked about. Then check dates, employer names and tools, because those are the details most often verified.

One profile, two languages

Many applicants need both an Arabic and an English resume. Enter your data once, generate in one language, then switch and generate again, or use the Translate action on a single section. Always have someone fluent read the translation before you send it, because a professional term can change meaning.
